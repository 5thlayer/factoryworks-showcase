FactoryWorks Showcase puts every mod of the [FactoryWorks](https://github.com/5thlayer/factoryworks) suite in one world, beside the tech and quality-of-life mods they are made to work with. It adds no recipes or content of its own: what you play is exactly what the mods do in any pack.

## The suite

- **Groundworks**: lay whole lines of blocks in one drag, previewed before you click
- **Beltworks**: Factorio-style grid belts for any tech mod
- **Pipeworks**: Factorio-style fluid pipes and tanks, where every connected run is one segment holding one fluid
- **Wireworks**: Factorio-style electric poles that power every machine in reach
- **Craftworks**: ask for an item, and every step is planned, paid up front and crafted over time
- **Fieldworks**: ore patches, oil fields and the Harvester that works them

## Alongside

Storage, backpacks and tech mods to build factories with, and the recipe viewers, maps and quality-of-life mods to find your way. The Relations tab lists every mod in the pack.

## Bugs

A bug in one of the FactoryWorks mods goes on that mod's issue tracker. A bug in the pack itself goes on [the Showcase's](https://github.com/5thlayer/factoryworks-showcase/issues).
